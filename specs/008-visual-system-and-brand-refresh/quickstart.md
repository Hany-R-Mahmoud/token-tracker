# Quickstart: Visual System And Brand Refresh

Required checks:

- the product has a chosen visual-system direction that is clearly different
  from AI Token Monitor and CodexBar
- the design system supports both light mode and dark mode intentionally
- Overview, Analytics, Menubar, and Leaderboard all feel like one product
- the dashboard includes richer graph vocabulary than the current baseline
- the menubar keeps glanceability while becoming more visual and premium
- the design uses color to clarify efficiency, waste, health, and uncertainty
- the logo works at wordmark, app icon, tray icon, and favicon scale
- responsive behavior is considered for major surfaces
- empty, loading, and signed-out states are designed, not left generic
- the OpenCode handoff is specific enough to implement without guessing the
  design system
- implementation evidence shows `agent-impeccable` was used for assessment and
  follow-up, not only for decorative suggestions
