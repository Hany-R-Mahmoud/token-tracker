# Quickstart: Rich Menubar Insights And Command Center

Required checks:

- the closed menu bar surface shows a meaningful spend total next to the app
  icon, or a documented native fallback if the runtime blocks direct text
- clicking the menu bar entry opens a rich dropdown command center, not a thin
  status list with a single dashboard button
- the first visible section communicates both consumption and effectiveness
- the popover contains compact visuals for spend and at least one effectiveness
  or outcome signal
- Overview, Analytics, and Team / Leaderboard are directly reachable from the
  menubar without forcing a generic dashboard-only path
- provider health and reset information are still visible and clearly labeled
- heuristic-only metrics cannot be mistaken for quota-backed truth
- no-data, partial-data, and signed-out team states are readable and polished
- the menubar does not expose raw prompts, transcripts, or other sensitive
  session content
- the menu bar surface updates cleanly on real local data and does not require
  restarting the app to reflect refreshed totals
- OpenCode execution evidence shows agent-orchestrator, agent-implementer,
  agent-tester, agent-reviewer, and agent-docs participation, with
  agent-impeccable, agent-debugging, and agent-security used where their slices
  apply
